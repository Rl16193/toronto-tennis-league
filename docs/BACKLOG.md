# Master product and engineering backlog

This is the single working register for every unfinished item found in the closed planning package,
the D1-D5 sprint evidence, the `dev-anuj` founder/engineering documents, validation records, and the
separate future-work register. Work keeps its permanent `BLG####` source identity. A new `TSK####`
or `BUG####` is assigned only when a backlog item is promoted into active implementation.

`FUTURE-WORK.md` remains the product-facing future register; its rows are mirrored here so this file
is the complete operating backlog. Closed rows are retained at the end for traceability.

## Status and promotion rules

- `Pending`: approved product direction that remains future work.
- `Backlog`: repository or validation work ready for prioritization.
- `Blocked`: requires a recorded product, platform, environment, provider, or design decision.
- `Closed`: repository evidence confirms the stated outcome.
- Promotion assigns the next globally available task or bug ID. After assigning the historical
  DC00 evidence IDs, the next available IDs are `TSK0051` and `BUG0035`; the `BLG####` and source
  references never change.
