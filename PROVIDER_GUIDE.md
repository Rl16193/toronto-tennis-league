# Stringer & Coach Guide — Confirming and Disputing a Job

This guide is for stringers and coaches with a provider account on the Marketplace (Services tab). It covers how to see who's redeemed your offers, confirm a job is done, and raise a dispute if something's wrong.

## Where to look: "Your shop"

Open **Marketplace → Services**. If your account is flagged as a stringer or coach, you'll see a **Your shop** panel above the offer catalog — this is only visible to you, not to players.

Each row in "Your shop" is one player's redeemed coupon, showing:

- **Name** — the player who redeemed the discount
- **Coupon code** — the code they'll show you in person (e.g. `RS-8K42Q`), plus which offer it's for
- Two buttons: a green **check** and an amber **Dispute**

## Confirming a job is done

Once you've done the stringing or run the lesson, tap the green **check** button next to that player's row.

This marks the coupon **used** — it disappears from your open list and moves into "Recently used" at the bottom of the panel. That's the whole confirmation step; there's nothing the player needs to do on their end.

**Do this only after the work is actually complete.** There's no "undo" from this button, so don't mark something used just to clear it from your list.

## Raising a dispute

If something's wrong — the player never showed up, the code doesn't match what they're claiming, or anything else — tap the amber **Dispute** button on that row instead of the check.

This doesn't cancel the coupon yourself. It flags it for the organizer, who reviews it and decides:

- **Approve** → the coupon is marked used (as if you'd confirmed it)
- **Decline** → the coupon goes back to active, still valid for the player to use

You'll see the outcome once the organizer has reviewed it — no need to keep checking back.

## Player-initiated cancellations

Separately, a player can request to cancel their own unused coupon (e.g. they changed their mind). That also goes to the organizer's review queue — you don't need to do anything for this case, it doesn't require your confirmation or dispute.

## Quick reference

| Situation | What to tap |
|---|---|
| Job's done, everything was fine | Green check |
| No-show, mismatched code, or anything wrong | Amber Dispute |
| Player wants to cancel before you've done anything | Nothing — that's between the player and the organizer |
